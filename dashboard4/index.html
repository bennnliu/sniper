Middle and high volatility strategy

The strategy is profitable on the test years but does not beat simply trading every day. It made — per baseline set with a Sharpe ratio of —, against — for all days with the filter everywhere, and it met — of the criteria written before the test. A simulated $50K Combine passed within 30 trading days — of the time against — for that benchmark. In the walk-forward year it passed —, below the coin-flip control at —.

The rule: trade the nine-setting NQ breakout ensemble in New York hours on middle- and high-volatility days only. Apply the logistic filter on middle-volatility days. Hold the baseline size on middle days, and on high days hold baseline times 1.28 divided by that morning's volatility ratio, so size shrinks as volatility rises. One baseline set is one MNQ per setting. Test years 2016 to October 2024 use out-of-sample filter scores, but the rule itself was put together after looking at those years. The walk-forward year (November 2024 to October 2025) has been looked at before. Costs are an assumed 1.11 NQ points per round trip; pass and payout rates are simulated. Research, not financial advice.

Method, definitions and what is measured, simulated or assumed
  • Volatility ratio: yesterday's close times 20-day volatility, divided by its own trailing 250-day median. Middle is between the lower-third cut and 1.2828; high is above 1.2828. Both cuts were fixed on development data in an earlier iteration and are known before the open.
  • Size on a high day is baseline times 1.2828 divided by the ratio, never above baseline. The test uses fractional sizes; live trading needs whole contracts, which matters most at small baselines.
  • The filter is the logistic regression trained walk-forward on all New York trades. It skips about the lowest-scored third of trades, here only on middle-volatility days.
  • The funded-account simulation applies Topstep's scaling plan to the baseline size, not the reduced size held on high days.
  • Selection: the choice to filter only middle-volatility days was made after seeing that the filter helped there and hurt in high volatility on these same test years. Test-year figures are therefore flattered; the walk-forward year is the fairer read.
  • Measured: trades and daily results on 1-minute bars. Assumed: 1.11 points per round trip per MNQ. Simulated: every pass, breach and payout rate.
  • Trials: this brings the project's count to 203.
