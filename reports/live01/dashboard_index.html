What was run, and what this does and does not show
  • Strategy, frozen: nine-setting NQ noise-area breakout (lookback 7, 14 or 28 days; band width 0.8, 1.0 or 1.2), checks every 30 minutes from 10:00 to 15:30 ET, long only above VWAP and short only below, flat at 16:00 ET. Plus the loss minimiser: entry only if price was already beyond the band 5 minutes before the check, a trailing stop at half an expected daily move from the trade's best point, and trades only in the first half hour's direction.
  • Data: Yahoo 1-minute bars for the five test sessions (Yahoo serves only about 8 days at 1 minute). The band's lookback and the 20-day volatility use Yahoo 5-minute bars from 15 Sep 2026; every earlier price the rule reads falls on a 5-minute boundary, so this is exact. No strategy was run on the Databento holdout year; it was only used to compare prices.
  • Costs: 1.11 points per MNQ round trip ($2.22), and again at double. Fills one minute after each decision at that minute's close.
  • News: no CPI, jobs report or FOMC fell in the week (CPI was 10 Nov, jobs 6 Nov, FOMC 8 to 9 Dec), so no day was skipped.
  • Not checked: Topstep's own fill prices, the TopstepX feed, and the Dec contract's front-month status on TopstepX. 23 Nov is excluded because its New York session had not opened.
  • Five sessions carry no statistical weight. The development record shows losing weeks like this one are normal for the rule. This is trial 227 in the project log. Research, not financial advice.
