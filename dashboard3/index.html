Volatility and contract size

Volatility sets the dollar risk of each contract, but it does not tell you when to trade bigger. The daily swing of one set rises from — on low-volatility days to — on high-volatility days, so the same loss limit carries fewer contracts as volatility rises. The edge per unit of risk is no better in high volatility, and sizing each morning to a constant dollar risk passed a $50K Combine within 30 trading days — of the time against — for one fixed size.

Nine-setting NQ breakout ensemble with the logistic filter, all New York hours, every volatility environment. One set is one MNQ per setting, at most nine MNQ. Filter scores are out of sample: test years 2016 to October 2024 use a model trained on earlier years, and the walk-forward year (November 2024 to October 2025) has been looked at before. Costs are an assumed 1.11 NQ points per round trip. Pass rates are simulated. Research, not financial advice.

Two cautions. In the walk-forward year no size and no sizing rule beat the same trades with the side chosen by a coin by more than simulation error, so nothing here is evidence the strategy passes Combines today. And the logistic filter, retrained on all days as asked, helped in middle volatility, did nothing in low, and removed profitable trades in high volatility. It is used here because it was requested, not because it has been adopted.

Method, definitions and what is measured, simulated or assumed
  • Size is quoted as MNQ held when all nine settings are in the market. Nine MNQ is one MNQ per setting. Sizes that are not multiples of nine are scaled results, not an exact contract schedule.
  • Size grids stop at the Topstep position limit: 50, 100 and 150 MNQ for the $50K, $100K and $150K accounts.
  • Simulation: days are resampled in blocks of five. Loss limit, profit target, consistency rule and minimum days follow the verified Topstep rules file. Monthly fees are charged every 21 trading days.
  • The best size in each table is picked from the same simulation that reports it, which flatters every row equally. Walk-forward rows in the sizing-rule table use the setting chosen on test years.
  • Single-environment simulations are hypothetical: real calendars mix environments, and the sizing-rule section is the realistic one.
  • Measured: trades and daily results on 1-minute bars. Assumed: 1.11 points per round trip per MNQ. Simulated: every pass, breach and payout rate.
  • Trials: one filter and five sizing rules bring the project's count to 201.
