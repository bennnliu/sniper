Monte Carlo of the Combine and the funded account

Simulated. Whole days are redrawn in blocks of about five from the chosen sample and replayed under Topstep's rules: the trailing loss limit applied to open losses, the profit target, and the 55% best-day rule. 6,000 attempts per row. The random-direction control takes the same trades with the side chosen by a coin, and is what no edge looks like at that size.

Definitions and what is measured, simulated or assumed
  • The rule: every 30 minutes from 10:00 New York time, go long if price is above the upper band and above VWAP, short if below the lower band and below VWAP, and exit when price comes back inside the band or VWAP. Bands are the open or prior close plus and minus the average move seen by that time of day over the lookback, times the width. Everything is flat by 4:00 PM New York time. Source: Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon (2024).
  • Measured: prices, trade entries and exits, open gains and losses, from 1-minute NQ bars. Orders fill one minute after the signal at that bar's close.
  • Assumed: 1.11 NQ points per round trip per MNQ for commission, fees and slippage. Double cost is 2.22 points.
  • Simulated: every Monte Carlo figure. Fees are $49, $99 and $199 a month for the three Combine sizes plus $29 a month for API access, and $149 to activate a funded account.
  • In-sample: June 2010 to October 2024. Out-of-sample: November 2024 to October 2025, which earlier work has already looked at several times. The final holdout year, November 2025 onward, was used once for the single-setting rule and is not shown.
  • Sharpe ratio: average daily result over its standard deviation, times the square root of 252, counting days with no trade as zero.
  • Deflated Sharpe ratio: the chance that the Sharpe is above what the best of 191 worthless rules would show by luck.
  • Worst open loss: the lowest point of the combined position's profit during the day, including costs paid so far.
